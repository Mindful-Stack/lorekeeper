**replace**). Ask who is retiring it (a named human, rule 2). Edit `status: deprecated` and append
`Deprecated YYYY-MM-DD by <name>: <reason>.` as the last Status line; `decided_by` stays as
recorded. Show, confirm, apply in its own PR.
